Best for First-Time Visitors Return To Ship On Time

The Antiguan Experience introduces cruise passengers to the island beyond the beach — a scenic drive through rolling countryside, a visit to a private home, a stop at a pineapple farm famous for Antigua's Black Pineapple, and a relaxing finish on a beautiful white-sand beach. It is one of the most popular culture-and-beach combinations from St John's.

  • ✓4-hour format fits most Antigua port calls with return-to-ship buffer.
  • ✓Scenic drive through Antigua's lush interior and coastal villages.
  • ✓Pineapple farm visit with insight into Antigua's agricultural heritage.
  • ✓Beverages and lunch included on standard operator packages.
  • ✓Moderate activity — some walking at farm and beach stops.
