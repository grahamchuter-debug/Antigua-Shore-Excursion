The Antigua Exclusive History and Culture Tour is a small-group journey to the island's historic south coast — Nelson's Dockyard UNESCO World Heritage site, the Dow's Hill Interpretation Centre, and Shirley Heights lookout with sweeping views over English Harbour. With a maximum of 12 guests, this is the premier history excursion from St John's.

  • ✓4 hours with small-group visits to key heritage sites.
  • ✓Nelson's Dockyard — restored Georgian naval dockyard in English Harbour.
  • ✓Dow's Hill Interpretation Centre with island history exhibits.
  • ✓Shirley Heights panoramic views over Falmouth and English Harbour.
  • ✓Easy activity level — short walks at lookout and dockyard.
