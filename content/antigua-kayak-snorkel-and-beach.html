Antigua Kayak, Snorkel and Beach combines three highlights in one cruise-friendly itinerary — a drive through lush rainforest and fishing villages, kayaking in the sheltered waters of Cades Bay, snorkelling over the famous Cades Reef, and time to relax on a beautiful beach. It is the signature adventure combo for active passengers.
